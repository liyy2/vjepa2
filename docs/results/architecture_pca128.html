The design idea in one sentence

If the 49 M-parameter attentive probe is over-parameterized for 248 training samples, force the head through a hard 128-dim PCA bottleneck fit on train, then read out only second-order temporal statistics. Capacity drops ~20×, the spatial axis collapses by averaging, and the head only has to learn an MLP over hand-engineered temporal features — exactly the regime small data favors.

Pipeline (one video → one UPDRS score)

0
Multi-clip sampling — one video → 12 segments × 32 frames
data

A ~10 s hand-task clip at 30 fps is partitioned into 12 overlapping windows of 32 frames each (frame_step=1, allow_clip_overlap=true). Together they cover ~94 % of the original frames.

Why: a 32-frame window at 30 fps is ~1.1 s — enough to see 1–5 tap cycles. Twelve windows give the head a temporal axis to compute statistics over; without that, "first 1 s" and "last 1 s" of the clip are indistinguishable.

V-JEPA 2.1 ViT-L encoder — FROZEN
frozen · no grad

Each of the 12 × B segments goes through the ViT-L backbone independently. Patch size 16 → 24×24=576 spatial tokens. Tubelet size 2 → 32/2=16 temporal tokens per segment. Embedding dim 1024.

The encoder returns last-block tokens only (n_output_distillation=1), passed through the corresponding norm.

Why frozen: 248 training samples can't fine-tune 300 M params without overfitting. Meta's attentive-probing recipe assumes frozen for the same reason.

ClipAggregation — concatenate the 12 segments along time
no params

The wrapper reshapes each segment to [B, T=16, S=576, D] then concatenates along the temporal axis (dim=1), giving one long token sequence per video. Sinusoidal temporal pos-embed is added on the fly so the head can tell which segment a tubelet came from.

This is the silent deviation from Meta's recipe (logit-averaging across K independent probe passes). For this stats head it actually helps: stats over a long sequence are richer than stats over 12 separate short ones.

Spatial mean pool — drop the spatial axis
no params

Reshape to [B, 192, 576, 1024], then take the mean over the 576 spatial tokens (spatial_pool=mean). One vector per tubelet remains.

Why mean-only (not mean+std): after PCA the head has only 128 channels to spend. Spatial std would double the input dim before PCA and force the bottleneck to spend variance on second-order spatial structure that a finger-tap rhythm doesn't need.

Pre-computed standardization — z-score per channel
no grad · cached

The head reads feature_stats_npz at construction, computes per-channel mean and std over all (sample × tubelet) of the cached training features, and stores them as non-trainable buffers. At forward time: (x − μ)/σ.

Why precomputed, not BatchNorm: global batch is 8. BatchNorm statistics over 8×192 tubelets would be noisy and inconsistent between train and val. Frozen stats from the whole train set are stable and identical across train/val.

PCA bottleneck — 1024 → 128
no grad · frozen

Sklearn PCA(n_components=128, svd_solver="randomized") is fit on the standardized cached training features (~250 × 192 ≈ 48 k rows × 1024). Components are stored as a non-trainable buffer; at forward time the head computes x · Wᵀ.

Reported on this run: explained variance = 94.27 % at 128 dims.

Why this is the load-bearing change: 128 dims is hard ceiling on what the head can read about a tubelet. The attentive probe could memorize each training clip through 49 M params; this head can't memorize because PCA strips the variance directions that don't survive on train.

Projection — LN · Linear(128, 128) · GELU · Dropout(0.175)
trainable

A small per-tubelet projection. LayerNorm normalizes per token, the Linear remixes the 128 PCA channels, GELU adds nonlinearity, Dropout regularizes.

Why same in/out dim: we don't want to expand capacity here — the bottleneck is the point. This layer is mainly a place to re-rotate PCA axes into something the temporal stats can use.

Temporal statistics — 30 vectors per video, no learning
no params

Pool the 192-step sequence into 30 fixed feature vectors of width 128, then concat:

mean+ std+ max+ first+ last+ last − first

plus 12 equal-length temporal bins:

bin-mean × 12+ bin-std × 12

Total: 6 + 12 + 12 = 30 vectors × 128 = 3840 features per video.

Why this set: tap rhythm/amplitude/decrement is a temporal-statistics problem. Bin means capture the trajectory; bin stds capture per-bin variability (regularity). last − first captures decrement — a known UPDRS marker.

MLP head — LN · Linear(3840, 512) · GELU · Dropout(0.35) · Linear(512, 5)
trainable

Two-layer MLP onto 5 logits. The 512-wide hidden layer is the largest single weight matrix in the head (3840 × 512 ≈ 2.0 M params).

Why dropout 0.35: the input 3840 is highly correlated (every group of 128 dims came from the same PCA features under different pooling). High dropout breaks those correlations so the second linear can't depend on any single bin.

Inference — softmax · expected-value or argmax
no params

Loss is cross-entropy. At eval time, softmax(logits) gives p(class); the eval loop computes both:

  • argmax → discrete prediction → accuracy / QWK / confusion matrix
  • Σ k · p(k) (expected ordinal value) → Spearman / MAE

Why both: earlier runs showed Spearman 0.3–0.5 while QWK was 0; the head's continuous output had signal but argmax was collapsing. The expected value is the ordinal-aware readout.
