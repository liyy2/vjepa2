4. Why coarse_step2 specifically

The R² scan (r2_kin_scan.html) showed that adaptive_step2 and coarse_step2 caches — both at frame_step=2, ~2.13 s/window — had the highest R² for predicting kinematic band-power and peak-count features. The 1.07 s/window stride=1 caches don't carry enough taps per pass for the encoder to expose tap-frequency content.

5. What this means in practice

  • V-JEPA is complementary to kinematic features, not redundant. Combining the two via ExtraTrees gives a stable +0.025 lift across 10 seeds.
  • The encoder's appearance bias doesn't fully erase rhythm-relevant signal — it just doesn't expose it the way decoders need for end-to-end accuracy. As a side-channel to a tree-based model that already has kinematic features, the V-JEPA spectral content (band powers, peak counts) sharpens borderline cases.
  • Best single seed (777): 0.7690. If we report mean ± std across the same 10 seeds, the honest claim is QWK 0.7499 ± 0.014.
  • Caveat: still single fold. The next step is the same 10-seed comparison on folds 1-4 to get a true CV estimate. Doable with the existing pipeline.
