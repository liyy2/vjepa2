3. What changed since the prior report

  • Bug fix in src/datasets/clip_dataset.py: adaptive sampler now uses linspace uniformly instead of i × clip_len with collapse-to-max-start. Eliminates duplicate segments at the end of long clips.
  • Three new adaptive caches: fine (K = ⌈duration_s⌉ max 48), coarse stride-2 (max 24), dense96 (min=32 max=96 — heavy overlap on short clips).
  • Long-training combiner sweeps with patience 900 (was 80), epochs up to 1500 (was 200). Best single configs now train past 350 epochs before val plateau.
  • Faster LR decay: cosine reaches 0.01 × base_lr by epoch 400 (vs 0.05 over full 1500 prior), then holds flat — gives more "settled" tail with very small LR for late-game convergence.
  • WandB enabled on new sweeps (per user directive).
  • Conservative-aggressive LoRA-FT in flight: rank 16, attn modules only (no MLP), encoder LR 5e-5, 30 epochs. Has reached probe QWK 0.499 at epoch 16, slightly above the prior LoRA-FT's 0.464 peak. Still 11 epochs to go.

6. What's been pushed (numbered)

  1. Adaptive K_i = ⌈duration_s⌉ per clip instead of fixed 12 segments — 100% coverage on long clips. Cache: 192–768 tokens per clip.
  2. Dense96 cache: min 32, max 96 segments per clip — forces heavy overlap on short clips, treats all clips uniformly.
  3. Multi-scale adaptive: fine (K_max=48) + coarse stride-2 (K_max=24) caches fused as parallel streams in combiner. This is the current top performer.
  4. Long training with high patience: 1500 epochs, patience 900, faster cosine LR (decay to 1% by ep400, hold).
  5. Cross-window combiner with 2D positional encoding (segment_idx, within-segment_idx) — replaces logit-mean across segments.
  6. Conservative-aggressive LoRA-FT: rank 16 (was 8) on attention modules, softmax + unweighted CE + label smoothing 0.1, encoder LR 5e-5, 30 epochs.
  7. Top-K probability ensembling across seed-diverse configs — average val_probs of top-K runs by QWK.
